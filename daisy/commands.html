Daisy Commands

Simple, clean, and powerful commands to control your music experience.

Music Commands

/play

Play music from a supported source or search query.

/pause

Pause the currently playing track.

/resume

Resume paused playback.

/skip

Skip the current track.

/stop

Stop playback and clear the queue.

/queue

View the current music queue.

/loop

Toggle loop mode for track or queue.

/volume

Adjust the playback volume.
