Daisy Commands

Simple, fast, and powerful commands to control your music experience.

/play

Play a song or playlist from a supported source.

/pause

Pause the currently playing track.

/resume

Resume paused music.

/skip

Skip the current track.

/stop

Stop playback and clear the queue.

/queue

View the current music queue.

/remove

Remove a specific track from the queue.

/clear

Clear all queued tracks.

/volume

Adjust the playback volume.

/loop

Loop the current track or entire queue.

/autoplay

Automatically play similar tracks when the queue ends.

/ping

Check Daisy’s response time.

/stats

View bot statistics and uptime.
